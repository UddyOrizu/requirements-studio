# Client Onboarding – KYC and Engagement Acceptance
**SOP reference:** OPS-ONB-004 · **Version:** 4.0 · **Owner:** Head of Client Onboarding · **Effective:** 01/03/2026

## 1 Purpose
This procedure sets out how new audit and advisory clients are taken on, so that the firm meets its anti-money
laundering obligations and only accepts engagements it is independent to perform.

## 2 Trigger
The procedure starts when an Engagement Manager receives a request to act for a new client. The Engagement
Manager logs the request in the CRM, recording the client legal name, company number and the requested service.

## 3 Customer due diligence
3.1 The Onboarding Team requests KYC documents from the client within one working day of the CRM request being
logged. The document set comprises certified photo ID and proof of address for each director and beneficial owner
holding 25% or more, and the certificate of incorporation.

3.2 On receipt, the Onboarding Team verifies identity documents using the approved electronic ID verification
service. Where an ID document has expired, the client is asked to provide a valid replacement.

3.3 The Onboarding Team screens the company and every director and beneficial owner against sanctions lists,
PEP lists and adverse media using the screening service. All screening results are saved to the client file.

## 4 Risk assessment
4.1 The Onboarding Team assigns a client risk rating of Low, Medium or High using the risk rating matrix.
A client is rated High where any director is a PEP, or where screening returns significant adverse media,
or where the client operates in a high-risk jurisdiction. A client is rated Low where there are no PEP matches,
no adverse media and the jurisdiction is standard.

4.2 High-risk clients must be approved by the Engagement Partner before the engagement can proceed. The Engagement
Partner should escalate promptly if they have concerns. Low-risk clients proceed directly to the conflict check.

## 5 Independence and conflicts
5.1 The Independence Team performs a conflict and independence check for every new client.
5.2 If no conflict exists and the risk is acceptable, the engagement is accepted. If a conflict cannot be managed,
the engagement is declined and the Engagement Manager notifies the client.

## 6 Engagement letter
6.1 Once accepted, the Engagement Manager issues the engagement letter. The whole onboarding process should be
completed within 10 working days of the request being logged.
